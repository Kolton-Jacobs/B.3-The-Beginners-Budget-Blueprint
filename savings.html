Start Small

Saving money does not have to begin with a large amount. Even saving $10 or $20 at a time can help you build the habit of setting money aside. The most important part is being consistent.

Beginner Savings Tips

  • Track your spending for one full month.
  • Separate needs from wants before making purchases.
  • Set a small savings goal that feels realistic.
  • Limit unnecessary subscriptions and impulse purchases.
  • Try to save a small amount from each paycheck.

Emergency Fund Basics

An emergency fund is money set aside for unexpected expenses, such as car repairs, medical costs, or a temporary loss of income. A beginner goal could be saving $500 first, then slowly working toward three to six months of expenses.

Pay Yourself First

Paying yourself first means setting aside money for savings before spending on non-essential items. This can help make saving a regular habit instead of something you only do with leftover money.

Quick Tip

Small choices add up over time. Cutting back on just a few unnecessary purchases each week can create extra room in your budget for savings or future goals.
