Example Monthly Budget

This sample budget shows how a student or young adult might organize monthly income and expenses. Your real budget may look different depending on your income, bills, and goals.

Category Amount
Monthly Income $2,500
Rent / Housing $900
Utilities $150
Groceries / Food $300
Transportation $200
Entertainment $100
Other Expenses $150
Savings $250
Remaining Balance $450

What This Budget Shows

This example shows that budgeting is not just about paying bills. It also includes planning for savings, leaving room for flexible spending, and making sure expenses stay below income.

How to Use This Example

Use this sample budget as a guide when creating your own. Replace the example amounts with your real numbers, then compare your income to your expenses. If your expenses are too high, look for categories where you can make adjustments.
