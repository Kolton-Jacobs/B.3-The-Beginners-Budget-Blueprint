# B.3-The-Beginners-Budget-Blueprint
The Beginners Budget Blueprint is a website designed for college students and young adults to manage their money efficiently through tools and educational guidance.

Financial literacy is more important now than ever, prices are at an all time high, whether that it housing, groceries, or utilities. The Beginners Budget Blueprint was created to help the young people moving out into the real world today. 

So far the tools that have used for this site are HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. I chose these tools because they are something I am a little familiar with, and they are relatively easy to understand.
